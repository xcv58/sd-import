SD Import ships as a free, signed, and notarized DMG for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or newer.

SD Card Import · Coming to the App Store

The App Store edition will support Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or newer. It is not available to download yet; the download button below is for the free direct edition, SD Import.

One successfully completed import is free. After that, a one-time $9.99 U.S. purchase unlocks unlimited completed imports and supports Family Sharing. Scanning and previewing stay free. Local pricing may vary. There is no subscription.

Once available, install SD Card Import through the App Store, then select your source and destination folders in the app. Apple handles installation, updates, and purchases.
