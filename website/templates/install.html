SD Card Import · Mac App Store

SD Card Import supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or newer. Download it on the Mac App Store.

One successfully completed import is free. After that, a one-time $9.99 U.S. purchase unlocks unlimited completed imports and supports Family Sharing. Scanning and previewing stay free. Local pricing may vary. There is no subscription.

Install SD Card Import through the App Store, then select your source and destination folders in the app. Apple handles installation, updates, and purchases.

SD Import · Free direct download

Download the DMG, move the app into Applications, then choose your import folders on first launch.

Requirements: Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14 or newer, and enough free space at the selected photo or video destination.
